# Methodology

The toolkit separates three layers:

1. **Syllabus** — a stable topic taxonomy used to organize preparation.
2. **Questions** — practice and PYQ records tagged by paper and topic.
3. **Learning tools** — frameworks that help users convert topics into revision and answer-writing practice.

## Data principles

When expanding the data:

- Keep topic tags consistent.
- Avoid duplicating near-identical topics.
- Record the year and paper for genuine PYQs.
- Prefer official or authoritative sources.
- Never imply that a sample question is an official UPSC question.
- Do not upload copyrighted coaching material.

## Goal

The project aims to make UPSC preparation more structured, searchable, and community-driven while keeping the underlying educational resources open and transparent.
